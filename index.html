About HAS Physiotherapy
Hospital expertise,
personal care

HAS Physiotherapy Centre in Nugegoda is led by a physiotherapist with 40 years of experience at the General Hospital Colombo — Sri Lanka's premier teaching hospital. After retiring as Superintendent of Physiotherapists, the highest physiotherapy designation at General Hospital, our principal physiotherapist now offers the same world-class care in a warm, accessible private clinic setting.

We specialise in pain management, post-operative rehabilitation, musculoskeletal conditions, neurological recovery and sports injuries. Every treatment plan is carefully tailored — no generic protocols.
